# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and constructive in discussions.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via
the contact listed in README.md. All complaints will be reviewed promptly.
